Musculoskeletal and Sports Physiotherapist

I'm a physiotherapist because movement has always meant more to me than physical strength. I've experienced first-hand how movement and training can change not only how you feel in your body, but how you think, feel and show up in everyday life. It helped me build strength, confidence, discipline and a healthier mindset, and I've seen the same changes in my clients: moving without pain, regaining strength, returning to training, or simply feeling more confident in their body.

Services

MSK Pain and Movement Rehabilitation

I help people understand and overcome musculoskeletal pain by identifying the underlying movement, strength, mobility and load-related factors contributing to their symptoms. Treatment combines hands-on techniques where appropriate with targeted exercise therapy, movement retraining and progressive loading to restore function and confidence.

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

From acute injuries to the final stages of return to sport, rehabilitation is built around restoring mobility, strength, capacity and sport-specific performance. Whether it's a muscle strain, ligament injury, tendinopathy or overuse injury, the goal is not just to get you pain-free, but to rebuild your physical capacity so you can return stronger and more prepared.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Recovery after surgery requires patience, precision and progressive loading. Rehabilitation is tailored to the procedure and your stage of healing, with a focus on restoring range of motion, reducing symptoms, rebuilding strength and gradually returning to normal activity, training and performance.

Back and Neck Pain

Back and neck pain can be influenced by movement habits, strength, mobility, workload and lifestyle. I focus on identifying what may be contributing to your symptoms and building a practical plan using exercise therapy, movement education and progressive strengthening so you can move with more comfort and confidence.

Posture and Movement Correction

Good posture isn't about holding one "perfect" position. It's about having the mobility, strength and control to adapt to different demands. Through movement assessment, mobility work, strengthening and postural awareness, the aim is to improve how you move rather than simply telling you to "sit straight."

Acupressure

Acupressure can be used alongside exercise-based rehabilitation where suitable. Details to be added by Dr Prerna.
